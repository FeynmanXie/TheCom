# The meaning behind The Companion Files

*What are these works trying to tell us?*

Research date: 2026-10-17. Scope: COM2601, COM2602, COM2603, ABYSS THRESHOLD OBSERVER, BENEATH, ABOVE, and SWIM. This report adds interpretation to the completed investigation; it does not replace its source-frame or audio records. Evidence IDs E01–E18 resolve in `meaning-evidence-matrix.md` and in the new website chapter. The executive summary is `companion-meaning-summary.md`.

## Executive finding

The corpus demonstrably places recognizable subjects in altered representations: a reflected zebra is described as the same but different; an Eye/performer construction returns with a changed card and interface; the same lunar image returns rotated, cropped, enlarged and recolored; status language interrupts the flow of pictures and sound. These operations make recognition and uncertainty part of the viewing experience. They do not uniquely establish a message the creator intended to deliver.

The account requiring the fewest extra entities is **organized audiovisual art whose observable method is representation and transformation**. These are compatible descriptions, not competing final answers. A broader fiction remains credible: older creator-authored descriptions explicitly address testers and CONTROL. However, the accessible sources do not connect that apparatus to a defined COM operator or demonstrate a repeated message-caused state change. A viewer-as-observer reading is meaningful criticism, with no recovered creator statement confirming that role.

## Method, sources, and limits

Three layers are kept separate throughout:

1. **Observation:** visible source frames, verified text, measured sound, official metadata, or bounded test results.
2. **Structural interpretation:** relationships supported by recurrence, difference, placement or correspondence. Structure is inferred even when its ingredients are confirmed.
3. **Possible meaning:** an artistic, philosophical or narrative reading. Its confidence cannot inherit the confidence of a decoded word.

Priority sources were read before extending the interpretation: `companion-system-model.md`, `companion-hypothesis-ledger.md`, `companion-master-event-timeline.md`, `companion-narration.md`, `companion-eye-card-analysis.md`, `companion-audio-forensics.md`, `post-com2603-analysis.md`, `post-com-audio-visual-forensics.md`, `source-provenance.md`, and `system-model-research-log.md`. Current `companion-transmissions.md`, `post-com2603-sources.md`, and the saved official metadata ledgers supply textual anchors. Later corrections supersede earlier summaries: WHITE LIGHT replaces the erroneous separate VOID LIGHT reading; REALIGN… and HEALIN… remain prefixes; source-visible pip counts do not make unreadable card ranks certain.

No original evidence was modified. No new frame-by-frame sweep or ASR run was justified: those would repeat failed discriminators without distinguishing intended meanings. The one new bounded audit reuses the existing metadata snapshot to ask whether an external work explicitly names COM2601 or COM2602 as ABYSS names COM2603. `Website/scripts/prepare_meaning.py` makes its selection reproducible and writes only website outputs.

The archived watch ledger contains 70 records, 63 accessible records, and 50 nonempty accessible descriptions. Of 53 decoded segments, 32 pass both warning-free decoding and exact re-encoding. Four of those contain COMPANION: the three COM self-labels and ABYSS's reference to 2603. No corresponding external reference to COM2601/02 was recovered in that exact subset. This is a coverage result, not evidence that missing companions do not exist. Seven watch records, undecodable segments, internal video text, and changes after the snapshot are outside it. Fresh official watch-page retrieval and targeted official-domain searches during this extension returned fetch failures or no results; they supply no fresh negative evidence. All official text claims below rely on the preserved primary-source snapshot.

Audio descriptions rely on existing signal measurements. The previous forensic environment could not audition the local soundtracks, so measured spectra and channel changes must not be converted into claims about hearing speech, instruments, captured surf, or an original slowdown ratio.

## A. Why do the same images return?

### Eye and magician: recognition followed by substitution

**Observation (E02, E18):** COM2601/02 probably share the giant-Eye composition and moving performer source. The first card visibly changes to one heart pip; the second probably shows a ten-diamond layout. The performer cuts to REGION BETA in one work and the London interface in the other. BENEATH has a different human eye and a circular effect; it is not a demonstrated reuse of that giant-Eye shot.

**Structure:** keeping much of a composition while changing its payload makes difference legible against a familiar frame. The two-case Eye→performer/card→interface sequence is stronger evidence than the Eye symbol alone. It is not a universal seven-work grammar.

**Possible meaning:** observation may be mediated or manipulable. The performer can evoke illusion: seeing a convincing display does not guarantee knowing its production. Another explanation is an effective recurring visual signature. Neither establishes an actual watcher, magician-operated machine, or card-selected destination. The production stage of the substitution is unknown.

### Zebra: reflection, memory, and identity

**Observation (E03, E16):** COM2601's clear narration explicitly describes a zebra's reflection and reversed orientation. COM2602's probable transcript includes looking for something it cannot remember, but wording and the ending are disputed. COM2603 speech is limited to probable islands. A COM2602/03 plains shot is probably reused.

**Structure:** repeated zebra imagery invites recognition even as processing and orientation change. This directly supports a recurring narrative device. It does not identify an individual animal or establish continuous character memory.

**Possible meaning:** the reflection makes sameness and difference an explicit subject. Memory or lost recognition is a possible extension supported more weakly by COM2602's uncertain narration. It must not become a confirmed amnesia plot. The reflection line is not a verified cipher key or a universal instruction to flip every image; the shared-shot orientation test contradicts that stronger proposal.

### Moon: continuity can reside in an image

**Observation (E04):** COM2602 and COM2603 share the same lunar base texture under rotation, scale, crop and recoloring. COM2601's red Moon has no established exact base match. BENEATH cuts from its eye to a Moon without a confirmed COM lunar master match.

**Structure:** a recognizable image survives changes to its form and position in the edit: Moon→code in COM2602, code→Moon in COM2603. This is continuity of representation, not evidence of the Moon physically moving or a shared fictional camera.

**Possible meaning:** recognition can persist through alteration; context changes what the same image seems to signify. The alternative is economical asset reuse or visual rhyme. The observations cannot decide between those intentions.

### Water, coastlines, forests, walkers, faces, and distortion

**Observation (E10, E13–E15, E18):** water/reflection recurs in COM2601, woman/water imagery in COM2603 and ABYSS, face/waterline imagery in SWIM, and coast/lighthouse imagery in COM2603/ABYSS. ABYSS also shows a forest/walker and a person facing a sea horizon. Its lighthouse shot differs from COM2603's. ABOVE's tonal layers change while its cloud/city-like treatment continues. SWIM sound changes also often occur inside continuing visual treatments. No literal surface crossing was tracked in the audited waterline windows.

**Structure:** boundaries recur as visible interfaces—surface/reflection, sea/land, clear/obscured, image/noise. A walker may offer a situated viewpoint; faces allow recognition to emerge from and disappear into processing. Forest/coast juxtaposition creates a change of setting without establishing one route or one traveler. These motifs have uneven distribution; not all seven works show every element.

**Possible meaning:** uncertainty may arise from how a world is represented rather than from a change in that world. Thresholds can be perceptual: a viewer begins to recognize a face in distorted material. Passage, exploration, unstable identity, environmental attention and estrangement are all possible readings. Their alternatives include associative montage and texture/composition choices. No portal, literal crossing, source identity, or unified journey is recovered.

## B. Why are there system messages?

Verified words can perform several jobs at once. The five proposed functions should be compared locally:

| Function | Supporting example | Counterweight / unresolved distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Fictional operational instruction or report | ALL SYSTEMS NORMAL, REGION BETA, LONDON SCENARIO SIMULATION and REALIGN… use institutional vocabulary; older official CONTROL fiction exists. | A status report is not necessarily a command. No verified COM operator, stable variable or repeated causal rule connects these messages to pictured events. |
| Description of the present audiovisual state | WHITE LIGHT appears on a near-white card; END OF LINE coincides with termination of one displayed border line. | These are local correspondences. They do not make every word a literal caption of the image or explain HORIZON POINT GAMMA. |
| Narrative or compositional marker | Final COM code exits emphasize changes of picture and spatial audio; COM sign-offs recur. | Ordinary cuts can have comparable audio jumps. A marked passage does not establish a fictional chapter transition. |
| Artistic or symbolic statement | NORMAL beside a storm, REALIGN… beside a stable Moon, and HEALIN… before still-fragmented images invite questions about the authority of labels. | Irony, instability or repair as a theme are readings, not recovered creator explanations. A storm may be normal under an unspecified system's definition. |
| Signal left open to interpretation | Damaged prefixes and unexplained interfaces leave referents incomplete. | Ambiguity is an observable effect. We do not know whether each gap was intended, caused by processing, or left unrecoverable in the available copy. |

The messages therefore suggest **an authority-shaped way of presenting information**. Their graphic form asks us to read the screen as a report, even when we cannot identify the reporting institution. That structural inference does not establish a deceptive authority or political allegory.

Specific limits matter (E06–E09, E17): REALIGN's suffix is unknown and no Moon displacement is observed; HEALIN is incomplete and the following footage retains corruption; END OF LINE is followed by another numeric line after 1.12 seconds; WHITE LIGHT does not persist as a post-message state; REGION BETA and HORIZON POINT GAMMA use different qualifiers and cannot be made successive states from Greek letters alone. An older TRANSMISSION ALPHA/BETA/GAMMA/DELTA family destroys the uniqueness of those bare ordinals.

## C. What can “Companion” mean?

**Confirmed relationship, unknown kind (E01, E12):** the [ABYSS description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jQfXMOSdWEM) exactly decodes to COMPANION PIECE 2603. The [COM2601](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2eSrFAMJY2s), [COM2602](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4HIj-9mU4Tw), and [COM2603](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qa5pby9LZhM) descriptions self-label as COMPANION PIECE TWENTYSIX ONE/TWO/THREE. Self-labeling is not a second work naming a counterpart.

The term **piece** gives a restrained reason to start with an artwork-to-artwork relationship rather than invent a companion character. ABYSS may supplement, revisit, contrast with, or offer an alternative perspective on COM2603. “Thematic counterpart” needs only a relation of subject or treatment; “alternative perspective” additionally proposes a shared referent viewed differently; “supplementary narrative” additionally proposes story information; a broad recurring audiovisual experiment requires no single story. None of these more specific types is confirmed by the label alone.

Existing tests found no validated same-shot match among leading COM2603/ABYSS identity candidates, no sustained exact audio reuse in the sampled screen, and no special alignment under a fixed normalized-time comparison. These bounded results weaken a direct transplant or synchronized shortened-edit account. They do not negate the named relationship or exclude nonlinear, local or more transformed correspondences.

Other catalog connections have different evidential kinds. COM2601 **confirms** reuse of the older WEATHER UPDATE graphic template with altered content (E05); that is a production relationship, not an official declaration that WEATHER UPDATE is “companion 2601.” Eye/performer and zebra reuse are **probable** production links. BENEATH/ABOVE/SWIM as one narrative progression, a unique Greek-state chain, named missing companions, and an exact number of required counterpart works are **unsupported as established relationships**. They may be proposed for future testing, but none should appear as a recovered node in the graph.

## D. What role does the viewer play?

**Observation:** a reader of this investigation can decode numbers, compare frames, recognize a reused Moon and distinguish an identifiable eye from an uncertain blob. This website additionally structures those activities. These research actions must not be confused with a verified rule built into the original artwork.

**Structure:** the films give uneven access to information: recognizable subjects coexist with unreadable text and damaged voices. Repetition lets a viewer form expectations; changed payloads can challenge them. The zebra narration explicitly discusses an observing subject, and the giant Eye provides an observation motif. These are concrete reasons to consider perception an active problem in the experience.

**Possible meaning:** “the viewer becomes the observer” is a useful hypothesis: the audience reconstructs connections while lacking the operator's supposed overview. It can also describe what a puzzle-oriented audience chooses to do with ambiguous art. E11's older [INFORMATION](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=695uky0yYLU) addresses TESTERS and CONTROL; it proves earlier creator-authored participatory fiction, **not** that every COM viewer has that role or that the giant Eye is the audience. The sampled COM comment/post audit recovered no direct explanation. Intended audience role remains unresolved.

The new chapter demonstrates this distinction with a frame choice: recognizing an image is an observation; treating its recurrence as a pattern is structural interpretation; assigning the viewer an observer role is possible meaning. The exercise illustrates a reading, not an experiment proving authorial intent.

## E. Four explanatory models

These IDs follow the new task. They are not the same numbering as the older nine-model ledger: new H1 = old organized H1; new H2 = old H4; new H3 = old H3; new H4 combines old H2/H8 and the viewer-role hypothesis. Models can coexist and have different scopes; their confidence is qualitative, not a probability of creator intent.

| Model | Support | Contradiction or pressure | Still unexplained | Extra assumptions | Prospective discriminatory value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1 Organized audiovisual art** | Shared ending, deliberate source reuse, varied sound/picture joins, heterogeneous processed media. | No positive contradiction to the broad organized version. A purely random account fits poorly; a wholly non-referential account ignores older explicit fiction. Neither stronger claim defines this H1. | Why these particular words and subjects? What kind of companion is ABYSS? | Fewest: deliberate composition, without an unseen machine or continuous character. | Low if it merely accommodates any edit. A future reused template is compatible with all four models, so its discovery alone would not distinguish them. | High as construction account; intended meaning unselected. |
| **H2 Representation and transformation** | Explicit reflection narration, altered card payload, shared lunar image, changed weather template, WHITE LIGHT's present-image correspondence. | Interface-only reuse and universal inversion versions fail; reuse also occurs away from interfaces. Broad theme survives. | Specific institutional vocabulary; why ABYSS explicitly names 2603; artist's intended message. | Treat transformations as thematically salient rather than only craft or branding. | A concrete unchanged-frame/changed-label relation could be predicted in a held-out source. Even success must beat an organized-art alternative; a motif resemblance is insufficient. | Strong structural reading; artistic thesis plausible, unconfirmed. |
| **H3 Fictional system or simulation** | Operational words, spatially coherent London markers, old CONTROL/TESTERS/RADIO fiction. | Tested universal cleanup, terminal-code, mono-code and color-restoration rules fail. These refute narrow mechanisms, not all possible fiction. | Operator, target, persistent variables, COM-to-CONTROL bridge, causal link from messages to events. | A coherent unseen apparatus and correspondence between edits and its states. | High only after a rule is fixed: the same qualified variable and exact message should predict a specific held-out response and distinguish a no-command control. | Possible general fiction; low support for any recovered operational model. |
| **H4 Observer, perception and threshold narrative** | Eye, explicit zebra observation/reflection, horizon/water imagery, ABYSS title, uneven recognizability. | Audited surface shots end in cuts without tracked crossings; the major ABYSS sound shift precedes the woman. A universal crossing/human-entry mechanism fails. | Identity of observer, continuity of subject, intended viewer role, why some major cuts lack sound cues. | Bind motifs into a viewpoint or journey; stronger versions require observer identity and a narrative. | A new direct creator role statement or repeated independently verified viewpoint relation would matter. More coastlines alone would not. | Supported theme; literal observer and continuous threshold story unconfirmed. |

H1 and H2 currently fit the observable record with fewer unsupported entities than H3 or a literal H4 story. They are not uniquely selected accounts of intention. Treating H1 as art without meaning, or treating H2's good fit as proof of the artist's thesis, would each exceed the evidence.

## What could change this account?

- An authenticated statement specifying what ABYSS contributes to COM2603 could distinguish counterpart, perspective, supplement, and experiment.
- A direct external reference to COM2601/02 in an accessible official source could add a confirmed named relationship.
- A stable qualified identifier and an exact, pre-specified message-response rule in held-out material could strengthen a literal system model.
- A creator statement addressing the COM audience as observer/tester could raise the viewer-role reading above interpretation.
- Better masters could recover damaged text or speech. Recovery of another motif alone would not resolve intent.

The strongest defensible interpretation is that these works make recognizable material unstable through its presentation, while system-like language offers an appearance of order. A broader fiction may coexist with that method. We can establish the transformations and propose why they matter; the accessible evidence cannot tell us exactly what the artist intended.
