# Post-COM audio and visual forensics

**Scope:** the four locally preserved official uploads [ABYSS THRESHOLD OBSERVER](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jQfXMOSdWEM), [BENEATH](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PpSA-AGIqCc), [ABOVE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gJTeoniCg4w), and [SWIM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bs8mrgXF6R0), with established COM2603 material used only as a comparison. The [source register](post-com2603-sources.md) preserves titles, descriptions, dates, hashes, and original compressed streams. This pass treats the local 48 kHz stereo WAVs as decoded copies of the available YouTube Opus soundtracks, not studio masters.

**Deliverables:** [complete 53-region audio event CSV](post-com-audio-events.csv), [audiovisual master timeline](post-com-audiovisual-master-timeline.md), [0.5 s measured features](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/halfsecond_features.csv), [audio boundary candidates](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/candidate_audio_boundaries.csv), [ABYSS 40 ms boundary data](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/abyss_40ms_178_218.csv), [ABOVE 2 s band-peak trajectories](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/above_band_peaks_2s.csv), [processed listening-test index](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/listening-tests/index.csv), and [exploratory transformed-audio retrieval results](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/transformed_audio_candidates.csv). The event CSV partitions the full 254.967 / 538.401 / 142.891 / 210.133 s decoded durations without gaps. Source frame indices use 25 fps.

The [33 selected visual anchors with 200 ms audio flanks](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/visual_anchor_audio_flanks.csv) reverse the check: known or visually recognizable image changes are each queried against the original soundtrack. Exact source-frame anchors are distinguished from approximate one-second-sheet events. For example, BENEATH's exact eye→Moon cut at 127.72 s follows a near-silent preflank and yields a **+32.85 dB** mid-RMS change, whereas ABYSS's exact woman appearance at 192.96 s changes mid RMS by only **+0.22 dB** over the same 200 ms comparison. These two cases argue against a universal human-entry sound cue.

## Method and limitation

[`post_com_audio_metrics.py`](../scripts/post_com_audio_metrics.py) decodes each full track to 24 kHz stereo float and computes mid/side RMS, L/R correlation, peak/RMS crest factor, power fractions across five frequency bands, spectral centroid and bandwidth, narrow peak prominence, and positive spectral flux for every 0.5 s. A standardized two-second flank contrast proposes change points; those are *navigation candidates*, not semantic or frame-exact edits. [`build_post_com_events.py`](../scripts/build_post_com_events.py) records integrated EBU R128 loudness and measured region medians. ABYSS's priority stretch was independently remeasured at 40 ms, one video frame per row. Original video frames immediately before/at/after priority boundaries are collected in [`av-boundaries/`](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/av-boundaries/) and [ABYSS's larger montage](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/abyss_boundary_frames/boundary_montage.jpg). These were visually inspected against the [one-second full-runtime sheets](../evidence/post-com2603/contact-sheets/).

**Audio audition limitation:** an attempt to deliver an actual 205–215 s ABYSS audio clip to this model returned “audio content omitted because you do not support audio input.” This environment therefore did **not** permit model-side listening. The earlier whole-track ASR produced no credible words and cannot replace listening. Labels such as tonal, noise-like, or possible processing below come from measurable signal behavior, not a claimed perception of strings, voices, surf, machinery, or reversal. This leaves the perceptual portion of the requested stop condition unmet. The [120 original/processed excerpts](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/listening-tests/index.csv) are prepared for a human audition; no word, melody, environmental source, or causal sound attribution is promoted from processing alone.

## Speed, pitch and reverse tests

[`render_post_com_audio_tests.py`](../scripts/render_post_com_audio_tests.py) produced original and reversed excerpts plus **0.25×, 0.5×, 2×, 3×, 4×** variants for ten source windows: ABYSS 183–199 and 211–223; BENEATH 109–131, 255–280, 420–438; ABOVE 0–24, 59–83, 113–137; SWIM 15–33, 108–126 s. For every factor, `atempo` changes duration while preserving nominal pitch, and `asetrate,aresample` changes pitch and duration together; the [index](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/listening-tests/index.csv) records every actual ffmpeg filter and file. Reversal is separate. This prevents a speed-up artifact from being mistaken for a recovered original, voice or tune. As audition was unavailable, these are **prepared tests, not positive interpretations**. No exact slowdown ratio is established by the ~64 Hz ABOVE drone: 4× playback would yield ~256 Hz, but the source could itself be a ~64 Hz designed sound.

## Cross-work source and reuse tests

[`screen_post_com_transformed_audio.py`](../scripts/screen_post_com_transformed_audio.py) screened ten 8-s probes against the other three full soundtracks using gain-normalized, locally whitened log spectra, time ratios 0.25/0.5/1/2/3/4, pitch shifts −12…+12 semitones, and forward/reverse ordering. This is a deliberately permissive *retrieval* screen; narrow drones can align by spectral shape while lacking source identity. Its largest lead, BENEATH **270–278 s** versus ABYSS **0–8 s**, scored **0.5004** at nominal speed/pitch. Direct 8-kHz waveform correlation is **−0.0006**, and the 100 ms RMS envelopes correlate **−0.448**. The screen's frequent near-ties between forward and reversed 3×/4× matches are another warning that static spectral similarity dominates. The lead is **not validated as reuse**. The prior [whole-track BENEATH/ABOVE search](post-com2603-analysis.md#explicit-inversion-and-cross-corpus-tests) found no straightforward reversed soundtrack (maximum |r| ≤0.1695 for its eleven fixed excerpts); the earlier end-of-work COM2603 comparison also found no exact common closer. **Transformed reuse remains UNRESOLVED, with no confirmed cross-work audio match in this pass.**

For footage, a high moving vantage over rows of building-like roofs is visible in **ABYSS ~30–40 s / f750–999**; an overhead road/vehicle and built setting occurs in **BENEATH ~36–69 s / f900–1724**. [ABYSS native proxies](../evidence/post-com2603/audio-forensics/abyss_early_visual_sheet.jpg) and [BENEATH one-second sheets](../evidence/post-com2603/contact-sheets/BENEATH_01.jpg) support an **aerial/built-environment category**, with residential function **UNRESOLVED**. ABYSS also presents forest/walker imagery ~70–90 s and later coast/person/lighthouse imagery. BENEATH begins with protective-looking figures and later favors blue eye/Moon/organic fields. ABOVE is cloud-to-city-like abstraction; SWIM is face/waterline→aquatic flow→dark contours/tunnel. Related categories recur, but no corresponding same-shot *and* same-sound pair is established. The established COM2603 woman/water and lighthouse subjects are context; ABYSS's lighthouse was already found visually distinct from COM2603's [earlier audit](post-com2603-analysis.md#explicit-inversion-and-cross-corpus-tests).

Web searches combining the channel/work titles with lighthouse, strings, music and aerial descriptors, and targeted generic stock searches, yielded candidate listings such as [Pexels lighthouse coast](https://www.pexels.com/video/woman-looking-at-a-lighthouse-8419065/) and [Storyblocks silhouette](https://www.storyblocks.com/video/stock/silhouette-of-a-woman-filming-the-ocean-and-lighthouse-at-sunset-with-misty-horizon-in-the-distance-filmed-from-the-back-with-no-face-349285807). Neither provides verified frame sequence, coastline geometry, or audio matching the locally preserved work. **No external underlying film, recording, stock clip or music source is identified.** The woman is described only as an on-screen figure, without identification by appearance.

## Audiovisual grammar and stopping judgment

Some edits are tightly synchronized: BENEATH's 9.16 s card and 127.72 s Moon, and especially ABYSS's 212.52 s sea→lighthouse cut with a 40 ms audio reset. Other major audio changes occur inside continuing images, including ABOVE's 60.5/122.5 s balance shifts and several SWIM/BENEATH texture changes. ABYSS's strongest mono/loudness move at 186.00 s precedes the visible woman; a bass change precedes her by ~1.68 s. Thus **selected audiovisual hinges are CONFIRMED**, while a fixed recognizable→abstract, human→environment, water→distortion, code→recognizable or observer/object reversal rule across all four works is **UNRESOLVED**. The ABYSS description's `COMPANION PIECE 2603` link is creator-declared metadata; a single grand narrative for BENEATH/ABOVE/SWIM is still **UNRESOLVED**.

The quantitative full-coverage and priority visual-boundary work is complete. The highest-value excerpts have separate pitch/tempo/reversal renders. **The required listening and positive source identification are not complete** because this model cannot receive local audio playback and the transformed retrieval did not validate a source. The evidence supports the measured edits and recurring audiovisual categories; it cannot settle strings, hidden speech, captured scene sound, source slowdown, original footage identity, or a single overarching story.
