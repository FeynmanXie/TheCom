# Eye, performer, and playing-card source-frame analysis

## Scope and source

This is a bounded original-frame examination of the two known Eye/performer passages. COM2601 frames **1597–1834** (63.88–73.36 s) and COM2602 frames **89–177** (3.56–7.08 s), all 25-fps frames in those intervals, were extracted as lossless PNGs under [`evidence/system-model/eye-card/`](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/). Five COM2601 and two COM2602 contact sheets index every frame; the numbered PNGs remain the authority for individual claims. The leads “Ace of Hearts” and “Ten of Diamonds” were held apart from pixel readings.

The exact pictorial content preceding the COM2601 heart has not been identified. Calling it a particular face, tarot card, or source film would exceed the pixels. ## Image continuity and reuse test

The reproducible comparison is [`scripts/analyze_eye_cards.py`](../scripts/analyze_eye_cards.py), with all measured values in [`card_frame_comparisons.csv`](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/card_frame_comparisons.csv). Between COM2601 f1771 and f1772, grayscale mean absolute change in the card interior is **59.72/255**, versus **11.40** in the hat and **20.45** in the hands. Card-interior correlation drops to **0.370**, while hat and hands are **0.741** and **0.811**. The next f1772→f1773 pair is essentially repeated image (card mean absolute change **0.006/255**). This is strong evidence for an abrupt *image-level card-face substitution*, not a witnessed physical card flip. It does not locate the editing stage.

SIFT features deliberately restricted to hat and hands, excluding card pixels, geometrically match selected COM2601 and COM2602 performer frames: **21/32**, **6/12**, **12/25**, and **18/27** RANSAC inliers/tentative ratio-filtered matches for four initially tested pairs. A later **temporal** test below strengthens the same-source inference. The card faces differ visibly.

### Cross-installment temporal alignment

[`align_companion_performer.py`](../scripts/align_companion_performer.py) compares every COM2601 frame **f1745–1790** with every COM2602 frame **f138–170**, using only normalized grayscale pixels in the hat and lower-hand regions, fully outside the card. Dynamic time warping of these two complete, ordered excerpts has normalized cost **0.1171** (lower is better). Reversing COM2602 raises it to **0.1874**; 1,000 complete random frame permutations have median **0.1638** and minimum **0.1431**. None scores as well as the original order. These are descriptive controls for temporal coherence, **not a p-value** or a population sample. The [full frame correspondence](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/performer_temporal_alignment.csv), [summary](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/performer_temporal_alignment_summary.csv), and [paired source-frame sheet](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/performer_temporal_alignment_pairs.jpg) preserve the result.

Selected matched frames advance coherently: COM2601 f1748→COM2602 f141 (masked correlation **0.936**), f1758→f150 (**0.921**), f1784→f164 (**0.903**), and f1790→f170 (**0.915**). An additional source-resolution [SIFT check](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/performer_temporal_sift_controls.csv) of these four pairs, again masking out the card, gives respectively **60/68**, **32/43**, **23/35**, and **35/55** RANSAC inliers/tentative matches. Deliberately mismatched f1748→f170 yields only **12/22**, and f1790→f141 **15/34**. The same hat, hands and torso geometry progresses in the same order while the visible cards differ. This supports **PROBABLE SAME MOVING PERFORMER SOURCE** across the two installments, stronger than a merely similar static composition. The degraded, differently processed copies do not yield a pixel-exact unaltered match, and the test cannot locate the source clip or prove whether the cards were replaced digitally, optically, or in an earlier source edit. COM2601's *within-shot image-level card-face change* remains confirmed separately.

## Hypothesis impact and limits

The two installments reuse a highly specific Eye→performer→card visual construction with different pip designs. The COM2601 within-shot card-face change directly demonstrates that the displayed image can change while its surrounding performer composition stays similar. These observations favor deliberate visual repetition and manipulation over a wholly arbitrary montage, and they are compatible with a media/representation interpretation. They do **not** establish a literal observer or simulation mechanism, assign meanings to suits/ranks, or prove a three-card sequence.

## COM2603 full-rate composition screen

[`screen_com2603_eye_card.py`](../scripts/screen_com2603_eye_card.py) decoded **all 12,427 original COM2603 frames** into 320×180 grayscale proxies and searched each full proxy at three sizes for four source-derived templates: COM2601/02 performer compositions and Eye compositions. All scores are in [`COM2603_template_screen.csv`](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/COM2603_template_screen.csv). A self-match positive control at the native position scores **0.993/0.993** for the two performer templates and **0.963/0.970** for the two Eye templates. COM2603's strongest scores were only **0.618/0.629** for performer and **0.593/0.617** for Eye. The [eight strongest separated candidate frames](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/COM2603_peaks/top_template_hits.jpg) were inspected at original resolution: they show unrelated night imagery, nature/glitch textures, a person in a doorway, numeric text over a light, and a corridor. None reproduces the known Eye or performer/card composition.

This is a **bounded negative for visible reuse of the known composition**, not proof that no small, redesigned, isolated, or heavily obscured Eye/card occurs anywhere in COM2603. The detector has not been calibrated for such alternatives. COM2603's apparent motif absence therefore remains **UNRESOLVED**, rather than CONFIRMED.

## Cross-installment Eye source-image comparison

[`compare_companion_eye_source.py`](../scripts/compare_companion_eye_source.py) measures a bounded grid of **828** COM1/02 Eye-frame pairs (every fourth frame of COM1 f1600–1740 versus every second of COM2 f90–134). It records centered grayscale correlation over the Eye/small-figure area and SIFT/RANSAC matches in the Eye area; the [full grid](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/eye_pair_grid.csv), [selected controls](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/eye_pair_controls.csv), and [paired frame sheet](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/eye_pair_controls.jpg) preserve the observations. COM1 f1704 and COM2 f124 have correlation **0.867** and **26/34** RANSAC inliers/tentative SIFT matches. COM1 f1676 and COM2 f134 have correlation **0.882** and **25/33**. The same pupil/lower-lid arc, central glint, and small figure arrangement are visible at source resolution. The degraded early COM2 f100 is a processing/visibility control, not a different-subject negative: its correlations with those COM1 examples are **0.007** and **−0.015**.

A separate [withheld-pupil test](../reports/system-model-research-log.md#2026-10-17--does-the-repeated-eye-display-different-destinations) asks whether the reused Eye visibly displays different observed destinations before the COM1 weather and COM2 London interfaces. Matching each of 47 COM2 Eye frames to COM1 by pixels *outside* the pupil yielded eight clear outer-Eye matches (correlation ≥0.65). Their withheld pupil correlations are **0.863–0.936**, median **0.924**, with no readable low-similarity pupil. [Per-frame scores](../evidence/system-model/eye-target-feed/matched_eye_pupil.csv) retain all weaker/degraded cases; COM2 f131 has a dark/obscured pupil and is not evidence of a replacement feed. A narrow literal Eye-as-visible-live-feed model is contradicted in the clear frames. Symbolic observation, editing and an unseen observer remain possible.

This supports **PROBABLE REUSE OF THE EYE COMPOSITION/BASE IMAGE** across COM1/02, alongside the stronger probable reuse of the *moving performer* sequence. The Eye test does not establish an independently moving shared eye clip, its original source, or any literal gaze/observer relation to the performer. Its co-occurrence with the performer may reflect a repeated edited audiovisual unit; the images alone do not settle its narrative meaning.

A corresponding [40-ms original-PCM audio comparison](companion-audio-forensics.md) found no alignment-specific waveform or spectral reuse under the matched performer frames. Thus the probable visual asset reuse does not presently imply reuse of the original sound from that asset.

## Six later works: complete known-composition screen

[`screen_related_eye_cards.py`](../scripts/screen_related_eye_cards.py) applied the same four source-derived templates at three scales to every decoded frame of PHASES OF FACES (3,053), STRINGS (4,940), ABYSS THRESHOLD OBSERVER (6,374), BENEATH (13,460), ABOVE (3,572), and SWIM (5,253): **36,652 frames total**. Complete score files and the five strongest separated original-frame hits per work are in [`evidence/system-model/eye-card/`](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/). The largest performer/Eye correlations, respectively, were PHASES 0.648/0.654, STRINGS 0.484/0.527, ABYSS 0.640/0.571, BENEATH 0.675/0.659, ABOVE 0.526/0.574, and SWIM 0.640/0.627. These are all well below the earlier original-frame positive controls (performer 0.993; Eye 0.963–0.970). Inspection of all thirty peak frames showed other faces, texture, coast/crowd imagery, abstract fields, and water highlights, not the COM2601/02 Eye→performer/card shot. This is a **bounded negative for that specific composition**, not for every possible Eye or card design.

## COM2603: bounded same-style card test

The [full-rate card-border test](system-model-research-log.md#2026-10-17--full-rate-com3-reuse-of-the-two-card-border-presentation) masked the COM1/02 cards' rank/suit interior and searched all 12,427 COM3 frames at three scales and arbitrary positions. Known card positives cross-match at 0.899–0.907. COM3 produced 461 scores ≥0.900, but the 33 temporally grouped peak frames and 30 separated top candidates show unrelated bright textures and scenes, not a card. The apparent white rectangular [f7901 candidate](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/com3-card-border-candidates/cluster_21.png) has a circular aperture and surrounding structure, not a pip/rank layout. **No known-style card is validated in COM3 by this screen.** The false-match rate and fixed border style prevent a general claim that COM3 contains no third card.

## BENEATH: distinct eye with circular overlay

The complete original-frame passage f2450–3224 (98.00–128.96 s) was inspected through numbered, consecutive full-rate contact sheets: [98–108 s](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/BENEATH_98_108_fullrate/) and [108–129 s](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/BENEATH_108_129_fullrate/). Selected frames were separately decoded as full-resolution lossless PNGs. At [f2748 / 109.92 s](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/BENEATH_native_sequence/f_02748.png), eyelids and lower lashes make the blue subject identifiable as a human eye, despite severe glitch. A bright [circular overlay at f2783 / 111.32 s](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/BENEATH_native_sequence/f_02783.png) develops into a larger, concentric, lens/ripple-like circle visible in the consecutive sheets around f2800–2850. Other image material intrudes through this passage, including [f2930 / 117.20 s](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/BENEATH_native_sequence/f_02930.png); do not treat the whole interval as an uninterrupted eye shot. The eye is again visible at [f3192 / 127.68 s](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/BENEATH_native_sequence/f_03192.png), immediately before a hard cut to the [Moon at f3193 / 127.72 s](../evidence/system-model/eye-card/BENEATH_native_sequence/f_03193.png).

Thus a **human eye, circular image effect, and eye→Moon cut are CONFIRMED source observations** in BENEATH. The eye is a different close-up from the giant COM2601/02 Eye composition; the template search did not find direct shot reuse. Calling the circle a literal camera lens, portal, observation machine, or causal transformation would outrun the evidence. The pre-f2748 onset is too corrupted for a secure anatomical boundary, and this local examination does not establish that no other Eye or card occurs elsewhere in BENEATH.
