The merged videos are 1920×1080 at 25 fps; ABYSS/BENEATH have VP9 video, ABOVE/SWIM AV1, all with Opus audio. Local FFprobe decoded both video and audio streams in every file. YouTube supplied **no manual subtitles and no automatic caption tracks** for these uploads at extraction, so no VTT files were created. The WAV is a lossless *decoding of the available Opus stream*, not an uncompressed original recording.

Every per-video local file (video, PCM, JSON, description, thumbnail, format list and identity snapshot) has a size and SHA-256 in [`evidence/post-com2603/source_hashes.csv`](../evidence/post-com2603/source_hashes.csv).
