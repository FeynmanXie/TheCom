# Final Companion corpus synthesis

This is a source-evidence synthesis, not a solution claim for unresolved strings. **CONFIRMED** means directly legible or reproducibly matched; **PROBABLE** marks bounded inference with missing source detail; **STRUCTURAL** marks an explanatory model. Audit paths: [timeline](companion-master-timeline.md), [transmissions](companion-transmissions.md), [narration](companion-narration.md), [commercial](commercial-forensics.md), [distortion](distortion-analysis.md), [provenance](source-provenance.md), [model comparison](semantic-models.md).

1. **Definitely common:** all three contain zebra imagery, numeric Trinary transmissions, extensive transformed/montage imagery, and the same `435351 = COM` audiovisual closing template. The closing video has aligned median frame correlations 0.99865–0.99895; corresponding audio was previously matched. Eye imagery is clearly common to 01/02, not established in 03.
2. **Messages:** 01 `ALL SYSTEMS NORMAL` **CONFIRMED** and Matrix `BETA SNOW STORM` **PROBABLE**; 02 city `LONDON SCENARIO SIMULATION`, `ALT SAFE HOUSE B`, and `HOTEL SIERRA` **CONFIRMED**, `REALIGN` **CONFIRMED prefix**, continuation U; 03 `END OF LINE` **CONFIRMED**, `HEALIN…` **CONFIRMED prefix**, completion U; all three `COM` **CONFIRMED**. Panel English `REGION BETA`, `SNOW STORM`, weather/status fields confirmed or bounded in the transmissions report. Other city microtext and the ticker remain partial raw strings.
3. **Zebra narrative:** 01 clear reflection narration, 02 probable night-sky/savanna narration, 03 short “first contact”/“gradually” speech islands. A recurring narrative device is supported; one continuous zebra/plot/voice identity is **not established**.
4. **Eye:** giant eye and small performer recur before other sequences in 01/02. Observation is a strong structural reading; literal surveillance or an identified observer is unproven.
5. **Magician:** top-hatted performer, hands and apparent die appear with the eye. Illusion/manipulation-of-seeing is a plausible structural role, but cards, trick mechanics, performer and original source are unidentified.
6. **REALIGN:** begins on frame 2819 immediately after blue Moon frame 2818. The Moon itself is stable to subpixel phase-correlation limits. A literal Moon realignment is **not observed**.
7. **City interface:** the exact numeric header and nine-frame Japanese predecessor independently identify `LONDON SCENARIO SIMULATION`; two boxed nodes decode `ALT SAFE HOUSE B` and `HOTEL SIERRA`. A Gherkin-like tower makes a City of London setting **PROBABLE**, though precise georegistration and source footage are unresolved. Boxed positions, X marks and dashed hub-like paths remain unassigned to surveillance, traffic, communications or travel. See [London reconstruction](london-scenario-simulation.md) and [breakthrough pass](com2602-breakthrough.md).
8. **Moon:** COM02 and COM03 share the same lunar base texture, with 03 rotated about 31°, enlarged about 1.45×, cropped and recolored. This is demonstrated by 28 geometric feature inliers. COM01 red Moon has no demonstrated exact base-image match. The lunar imagery's literal purpose is U.
9. **Oral-care source:** original advertisement(s), brand, year and country are **NO MATCH / unresolved** after topical web/image searches. There may be several spliced source shots.
10. **Commercial pipeline:** **REAL SOURCE + GLITCH PROBABLE**. Cleaner consecutive frames preserve plausible people and brushes; strongest anomalies track tear/chroma/persistence effects. AI video-to-video is possible; fully AI generation is unsupported.
11. **Faces:** apparent face/mouth duplication and transfer occur in distorted bands; intentional transfer from one specific person to another is **not demonstrated**. No reliable anatomical instability persists in a clean island that forces an AI explanation.
12. **Source material:** the 02/03 Moon is an exact *internal* same-base match, and all three closers are exact *internal* audiovisual-template matches. 01/02 eye/performer reuse is probable at composition level. No external wildlife, oral-care, city, corridor, crowd, water-woman, lighthouse or Moon master was positively identified.
13. **“Same but different”:** **STRUCTURAL, strongly supported** as repeated invariant subjects/templates with altered runtime, color, orientation, message, environment and corruption. It need not be a literal authorial instruction.
14. **“Up is down, left is right”:** water reflection, kaleidoscopic/symmetric zebra and 02 upside-down zebra provide actual visual analogues. No unique axis-reversal cipher solution has been verified; audio-channel swaps likewise did not produce a specific message.
15. **Climate change:** **unsupported as a specific thesis**. Snowstorm and environmental fields are real evidence, but no direct warming/carbon/emissions/long-term trend/human-cause evidence was found. Weather alone does not establish climate change.
16. **Broader environment/Earth:** **supported as a content domain** by wildlife, savanna, water, weather and city/humans, alongside celestial and abstract material. It does not explain all formatting/codes.
17. **Observation/perception:** **stronger structural explanation** than climate alone: eye, explicit observation narration, reflection, illusion figure, interface, reversed images and glitch all independently align.
18. **SYSTEM/WORLD:** a useful **structural hypothesis**: messages/status/COM and interface form a system-like layer; wildlife/weather/city/human media/Moon form observed subjects. The source does not specify an operator or prove the system causes world changes.
19. **Unresolved:** exact missing ciphertext (`REALIGN` suffix, `HEALIN` ending), other city node/ticker digits, possible additional tiny transmissions, full COM03 voice, external source clips and edit pipeline, function of the city graphic and route endpoints, exact city-shot registration, identity/intent of the observer if any. See [unsolved.md](unsolved.md).
20. **Narrowest model:** an organized, code-bearing audiovisual remix that juxtaposes observation of natural/human scenes with transformed and corrupted representations, recurring zebra narration and system-style messages, tied by the same COM sign-off. A literal system observing a planet is plausible but remains interpretation; specific climate narrative and named surveillance system require evidence absent here.

The evidence deliberately preserves partial strings and negative source searches. A future clean master, creator stem or exact external same-shot match could change several classifications without changing the confirmed corpus links.
