## Continuity test

The 01 narrator explicitly describes a zebra observing its reflection; the 02 voice describes a zebra looking at the sky and an uncertain future in a savanna. Both use third-person natural-history narration, and both are placed over zebra imagery. That supports a **recurring zebra-narration device**. The videos do not identify an individual zebra, reuse of the same wildlife master has not been shown, and the 03 voice cannot be transcribed sufficiently to demonstrate continuous plot or shared character. “Self-recognition,” “searching,” “contact,” “navigation,” and “learning” are useful interpretation hypotheses, not recovered words.

The 01 sentence `Up is down, left is right` corresponds visually to reflection and later kaleidoscopic reversals. COM2602 contains a visibly upside-down zebra head near 165 s. No unique ciphertext decode has been produced by reversing an axis, so this is a supported visual/thematic relation, **not a verified cipher key**.
