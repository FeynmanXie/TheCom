## Transmission found in BENEATH

At **9.16–10.00 s, source frames 229–250**, a nearly white card contains **two vertical numeric lines** along the left edge. The complete interval's original frames are under [`beneath_numeric_8_11/`](../evidence/post-com2603/beneath_numeric_8_11/); [frame 235](../evidence/post-com2603/beneath_numeric_8_11/f_0235.png), its [rotation](../evidence/post-com2603/beneath_numeric_8_11/numeric_f0235_rot270_2x.png), and a [six-frame source comparison](../evidence/post-com2603/beneath_numeric_8_11/overlap_source_frames_sheet.png) show the cells. The upper visible line is `35132163121502131613432`; the lower is `32163121502131613432312`. **The lower line's first 20 digits exactly equal the upper line's last 20 digits**: it starts at upper offset three and contributes the visible final `312`. Their exact union is `35132163121502131613432312`, which the pinned historical decoder reads **`WHITE LIGHT`** and re-encodes identically, with no warning or supplied digit. [Reproducible overlap and round trip](../evidence/post-com2603/beneath_white_light_overlap.json). **`WHITE LIGHT` is CONFIRMED as the combined source ciphertext.** The earlier `WHITE LIGH` prefix was incomplete only because each row was decoded separately. The lower row's standalone `V?D?LIGHT` arose from decoding a shifted fragment at the wrong stream origin; it is **REJECTED as a separate message**, including the attractive `VOID LIGHT` completion. This does not rule out the word `VOID` elsewhere, including SWIM's independently decoded description. OCR sometimes misreads individual glyphs, so the overlapping source-visible strings, not OCR grammar, establish the result. The visual mechanism producing two offset rows remains unproved.

A source-frame brightness test separates **caption of the present image** from a proposed later state change. In all **22 code frames f229–250**, more than 90% of pixels have luma above 220/255; no frame in the audited f200–228 or f251–274 flanks reaches that threshold. The card begins at f229 and cuts away at f251. [Per-frame values](../evidence/post-com2603/beneath_numeric_8_11/white_light_luma_f200_f274.csv) and [flank sheet](../evidence/post-com2603/beneath_numeric_8_11/white_light_flank_sheet.jpg). The words thus coincide with an actual white field. A **sustained white-light state after the message is rejected in these flanks**; the text may label the momentary graphic or a fictional momentary event. Neither reading proves a system operation.

No other **validated** in-video Trinary, Matrix, COM card, status display or spoken transmission emerged from the contact-sheet and detector review. This is a limited negative, not a claim that all sub-second overlays are absent. Unprimed offline ASR on each full PCM track produced no credible speech transcript; its only output was BENEATH `Thank you for watching` over a 60-second span of effects/music, a likely model hallucination. [Raw ASR JSON](../evidence/post-com2603/PpSA-AGIqCc_unprimed_asr.json) is retained rather than promoted to speech evidence. No manual or automatic YouTube caption tracks were available.

## Explicit inversion and cross-corpus tests

The `BENEATH → ABOVE → SWIM` publication order is **confirmed** (2026-06-06, 2026-06-25, 2026-10-12), but the words alone do not demonstrate a spatial instruction. A one-second sample comparison of BENEATH and ABOVE with 64-bit perceptual hashes under identity, vertical flip, horizontal flip and 180° rotation found nearest distances **14, 16, 14, 16 of 64 bits**, respectively (median pair distance 32). The strongest pairs had 0, 6, 4 and 0 geometric SIFT inliers when rechecked. These weak, processed-image matches do **not** establish shared footage under inversion. The test is sampling-limited and does not prove no such frame exists. COM01's spoken `Up is down, left is right` remains a documented visual/thematic rule within COM01 and COM02; its use as a decoding or edit rule for these later uploads is **unverified**.

A later full-rate [visual orientation screen](../reports/companion-boundary-analysis.md) also found no validated simple same-shot BENEATH/ABOVE flip among its leading candidates. An independent [whole-track audio search](../evidence/system-model/beneath-above-audio/window_matches.csv) tested eleven ABOVE eight-second excerpts against every BENEATH offset, forward and reversed. All ABOVE self-checks were r = 1.000; BENEATH peaks were |r| ≤ 0.1695, comparable to an unrelated SWIM control. A straightforward reversed BENEATH soundtrack is rejected. This bounded test does not exclude short, altered cues or a thematic relationship.

**Later full-rate correction:** A subsequent [all-frame orientation screen](companion-boundary-analysis.md) lowered the nearest hash distances to 10/12/12/12 (identity/vertical/horizontal/180°) by including every source frame. Native-frame checks of 24 leading distinct pairs still found no valid same-shot geometry; the strongest nominal SIFT fit collapses into a degenerate mapping. The earlier one-second result remains a historical sparse screen, superseded for this particular whole-frame search. Strong crops or nonlinear alterations remain outside the negative result.

The ABYSS [lighthouse at 215 s](../evidence/post-com2603/abyss_lighthouse_215.png) and the COM03 [lighthouse near 419.4 s](../evidence/companion/keyframes/COM2603_statue_419.4.jpg) differ in tower structure, surroundings and composition. SIFT produced 12 RANSAC inliers from 73 tentative pairs on sky/coast geometry, insufficient against the clear scene mismatch; **same source shot rejected**. BENEATH's Moon and SWIM's water face are likewise unconfirmed source matches to earlier Moon/woman frames. No zebra shot, earlier giant-Eye composition, coded status interface, `REALIGN`, `END OF LINE`, `HEALIN…`, or final visual `COM` marker was positively recovered in these four works.

The final 12 seconds of each later audio track were cross-correlated against COM03's final 12 seconds at 8 kHz mono. Maximum absolute whole-window-normalized correlations were **0.0263** ABYSS, **0.0576** BENEATH, **0.0088** ABOVE, **0.0205** SWIM. None supports reuse of the Companion closing audio. A soundtrack match elsewhere was not tested.
