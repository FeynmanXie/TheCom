# What is going on in *The Companion Files*?

This is my evidence-based reading of seven audiovisual works by **3121534312**: three *Companion* pieces (COM2601, COM2602, COM2603), followed by *ABYSS THRESHOLD OBSERVER*, *BENEATH*, *ABOVE*, and *SWIM*. They mix altered footage, recurring images, distorted sound, and strings of numbers that can be decoded. The strange part is that some details repeat exactly, while others return changed.

The short version: these works are carefully assembled around **seeing, representing, and transforming things**. There are status screens and simulation labels, but I have not found proof that they depict a real working system, a single continuous plot, or an identified observer. Here is what the pictures and sound actually give us.

## The three Companion pieces

**COM2601** introduces a huge, processed Eye and a top-hatted performer holding a card with one heart symbol. Later, a weather-like screen reads `REGION BETA` and `SNOW STORM`. A separate numeric message decodes to **ALL SYSTEMS NORMAL**. That contrast is intriguing, but we do not know *what* is supposedly normal. The message is followed by a red Moon. A zebra then appears with its reflection in water; the clear narration describes the animal seeing an image that is the same but reversed. The zebra is a visual example of the work's central trick: familiar material can look different when its presentation changes.

**COM2602** begins with a similar Eye and performer composition, but the card now appears to carry ten diamond symbols. A dark aerial city view has outlined boxes, markers, and dashed paths laid over it. Its header decodes to **LONDON SCENARIO SIMULATION**; two boxed labels read **ALT SAFE HOUSE B** and **HOTEL SIERRA**. A tower resembling London's Gherkin supports the city identification, though the exact footage source is unknown. These are *labels on an image*, not proof of a real safe house, hotel route, or live surveillance operation. Later, an upside-down zebra echoes the first film's reversal idea. A blue Moon is immediately followed by a message beginning **REALIGN…**. The remaining characters cannot be read, and the Moon does not visibly shift in that shot.

**COM2603** is a longer, more degraded montage. Its blue numeric screen says **END OF LINE**. A damaged red message shortly afterward yields only **HEALIN…**; completing it as “HEALING” would be a guess. A yellow-green Moon appears after the code, and a heavily processed zebra returns near the end. The Moon in COM2602 and COM2603 is demonstrably the same underlying lunar image, transformed through rotation, crop, scale, and color. An exact image match has not been established for COM2601's red Moon.

All three Companion pieces end with the same audiovisual closing template. Its digits, `435351`, decode to **COM**. That shared ending, the zebra, and the numeric messages tie the pieces together as a constructed series. They do not, by themselves, tell us that one character moves through all three films.

## Why the repeated pictures matter

The Eye and top-hatted performer in the first two films are probably made from the same underlying composition, but the cards differ and the sequences lead to different displays. The zebras recur as reflection, inversion, and damaged close-up. The Moon returns in red, blue, and yellow-green treatments. These are concrete examples of **same source or subject, different presentation**.

It is reasonable to read the Eye as a symbol of observation, the performer as a hint about illusion, and the zebra reflection as a question about what an image does to its subject. Those are interpretations. We have not identified a literal watcher, proved that the performer is controlling the images, or recovered a hidden rule that turns each image into the next.

## ABYSS: the sound changes *before* the face

*ABYSS THRESHOLD OBSERVER* has the clearest official link to the Companion series: numbers in its creator-provided description decode to **COMPANION PIECE 2603**. Visually it moves through abstract optical shapes, a high view over building-like forms, dark woodland with a walking figure, water or coast imagery, a woman in the sea sequence, and a lighthouse. These are descriptions of what appears on screen; the original footage sources and the woman's identity have not been established.

The key sequence is around **186–213 seconds**. At **186.00 s**, while the image is still an abstract water-like form, the sound gets much louder and changes from a wide stereo texture to almost identical left and right channels: it feels more centered and forceful. At **191.28 s**, the higher-frequency material falls away and a dense, bass-heavy sound takes over. The woman's face becomes clearly visible only at **192.96 s**. There is no matching hard sound onset at the instant her face appears. So the dramatic audio turn starts *before* the recognizable person, rather than being a simple “woman appears, sound begins” cue.

The sea and rock passage later grows quieter. At **212.48 s** there is a roughly **40 millisecond near-silent reset**; at **212.52 s**, the first lighthouse image arrives with a wider, fuller sound. The sustained lighthouse/shore section measures about **10.5 loudness units** above the preceding sea/rock section. That exact picture-and-sound cut is a real edit. It does not tell us whether the sound was recorded at a coast, comes from a particular instrument, or represents a fictional event.

## BENEATH, ABOVE, and SWIM

**BENEATH** has a creator-description code reading **HORIZON POINT GAMMA**. An early vertical number display reconstructs to **WHITE LIGHT**. An older “VOID LIGHT” reading was superseded when the overlapping rows were checked. The pictures include protective-looking figures, a blue processed human eye with visible eyelids and lashes, and a Moon. At **127.72 s**, the Eye cuts directly to the Moon; the measured sound level jumps sharply across that edit after a nearly silent moment. This is another genuine audiovisual hinge, though many other sound changes in the film occur without such a clear picture cut. “Gamma” does not prove an ordered Alpha–Beta–Gamma journey through the works.

**ABOVE** is visually sparse: cloud-like and city-like forms remain abstract, leaving the soundtrack especially exposed. A low component near **63–65 Hz** persists with a roughly doubled harmonic, while a higher tonal line shifts around **225–310 Hz** with another line roughly twice its frequency. Around **60.5 s**, the upper frequencies become more prominent; after roughly **122.5 s**, the deep bass drops while some higher components remain. The picture does not make a matching hard cut at those points. This supports layered or selectively processed pitched sound. It does **not** identify a violin, reveal an original playback speed, or establish a hidden melody. The site's faster, pitch-preserving, pitch-shifting, and reversed excerpts are listening experiments, not recovered originals.

**SWIM** starts with a face at a waterline. The image gives way to luminous aquatic flow, then darker contour-like forms. Its creator-description code reads **VOID**. Strong sound changes occur at several points, usually while the visual treatment continues rather than on the strongest picture cut. The word `VOID` does not restore the rejected “VOID LIGHT” reading in *BENEATH*, and the dark contours do not prove that we are looking at a literal tunnel.

## My reading, and the limit of it

The safest account is an organized audiovisual remix: images of eyes, animals, cities, water, people, and the Moon are repeatedly shown through interfaces, distortion, reversal, and recoloring. The decoded phrases make the work *feel* operational—`ALL SYSTEMS NORMAL`, `LONDON SCENARIO SIMULATION`, `REALIGN…`, `END OF LINE`—but the most explicit city label actually says **simulation**. Some sound changes coincide with visual cuts; other major changes happen while the image treatment continues.

An observer or fictional system may be part of the intended story. That remains a possible reading, not a decoded fact. What is solid is the repeated material, the verified messages, the measured sound changes, and the points where the evidence stops. Missing code endings, unknown source clips, and the creator's intent are still open.
